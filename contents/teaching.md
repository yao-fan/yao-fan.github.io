My enthusiasm for teaching is one of the main reasons why I decided to return to the academia. In the past, I have taught the following courses:

- **Concepts of Human Nature**, University of Pittsburgh (2020–2021), Graduate Teaching Assistant. Selected writings by Plato, Hobbes, Rousseau, Marx, and Freud, focusing on the historical development of conceptions of human nature.
- **History of Modern Philosophy**, University of Pittsburgh (2020–2021), Graduate Teaching Assistant. Selected writings by Descartes, Locke, Hume, and Kant, with a focus on epistemology.
- **Intermediate Logic**, Australian National University (2017), Graduate Teaching Assistant. The semantics and syntax of propositional and first-order logic, with an emphasis on proofs and logic programming.
